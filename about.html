About Our Organization

Our Story

Founded in 2012 by a small group of local animal advocates, Twin Cities Animal Rescue has grown from a grassroots network into a fully operational shelter and community outreach organization dedicated to animal welfare.

Core Values

  • Compassion: Treating every animal with dignity, love, and top-tier medical care.
  • Transparency: Maintaining open communication with adopters, donors, and our community.
  • Action: Responding rapidly to animals in critical need across the region.

Partner Acknowledgments

We are deeply grateful to our local veterinary partners, pet supply sponsors, and community businesses who donate food, supplies, and services to keep our shelter running successfully.
